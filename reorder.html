Reorder a thread by your bridging metrics

X orders a thread mostly by engagement. A bridging metric orders it by something else: what helps one side understand the other. Build up to three metrics from the features below, label comments by their bridging quality yourself, and compare the orders side by side. Your metric is formalised once its weights and its definition say exactly how it orders comments.
