1 · Pick a disputed post

The posts where the professional fact-checker and the community notes reached opposite conclusions: a verdict of false or misleading while the notes say the post is fine, or a verdict of true while the notes call it misleading. Pick one, press Work on this post, and download its thread: the original post, the fact-check, every community note and every collected reply, one row each.

The Statement column holds the statements the Datasets found in each row; several in one cell are separated by |. Statements exist only for the posts whose replies were collected: pick one of those, since you will close-read the thread by statement. A post's language is the language most of its replies are in.
