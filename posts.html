Disputed posts

The posts where the professional fact-checker and the community notes reached opposite conclusions: a verdict of false or misleading while the notes say the post is fine, or a verdict of true while the notes call it misleading. Pick one, and download its thread: the original post, the fact-check, every community note and every collected reply, one row each.

A post's language here is the language most of its replies are in. The Statement column holds the statements the Datasets distilled for the post: several in one cell are separated by |. Statements exist only for the posts whose replies were collected.
