1 · Select a disputed post

Choose a case and press Work on this post. A post is disputed when the fact-checker's verdict and the community notes reach opposite conclusions. Then briefly identify the main disagreement on 1 The disagreement.

The full thread can be downloaded as a CSV. Each row is the original post, the fact-check, a community note or a reply (Type); text_translated is the English text, like_count the likes, timestamp the date, and Statement the statements the row makes (several separated by |). A post's language is the language most of its replies are in.
