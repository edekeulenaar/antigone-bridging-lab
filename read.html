2 · Close read the thread, statement by statement

Users — fact-checkers, community notes and commenters — disagree around competing statements: expressions of an attitude, belief or opinion. Below, the thread is laid out by the statements its comments make. Open each statement and read the comments that make it.

…or open a CSV of your own (with a Statement column)

Questions to answer

  1. The majority. Which statements do most comments make? Which kinds of users make them: the post, the fact-checker, the notes, the replies?
  2. The minority. Which statements do few comments make? Are they rare because few users hold them, or because they receive little attention in the thread?
  3. Misunderstanding. Where does one side read the other's normative statement ("this should not be platformed") as a factual one ("this is false"), or the reverse?
  4. Misrepresentation. Where does one side attribute to the other a position it does not hold?
  5. What is ignored. Which statements of one side get no answer from the other?
  6. The premise. What would both sides need to share to be arguing about the same thing — and do they share it?

For each answer, note two or three comments as evidence (their author and a phrase). Base your bridging metrics on these answers.
