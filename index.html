What is in each thread's files

full_thread.csv · one row per item

The original post, the fact-check(s), every community note and every collected reply. Type says which. text is the original wording, text_translated its English translation. Notes carry their classification, note_status and rating counts; replies their reply_level and likes.

The website's coding: statements lists the numbers of the statements the item makes, statement_text their wording, and opinion_group the item's group in the opinion network (users who make similar statements). Empty statement columns mean the item makes none of the statements in the statement map.

statements.csv · one row per statement

The statements distilled for the post, numbered: their wording, the statements each contradicts, whether the post makes it, and how many fact-checkers, notes and replies make it. It is the website's codebook: use it as it is, or as the starting point for your own.

The statements were distilled by hand from the fact-check article, the most-rated notes and the most-liked direct replies; every other note and reply was assigned to a statement by text similarity (precision about 58%, recall about 63% against the hand coding). Treat the coding as a first pass, not as ground truth.
