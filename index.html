Assignment 3 — Bridging: From Disagreement to Intervention

Deadline: Monday, 7 December, 17:00.

Using this bridging lab, explore how the ordering of comments might facilitate understanding between people who disagree.

Your task is to:

  1. Select a disputed post

    Choose a case from the bridging lab and briefly identify the main disagreement.

    1 Select a post → · 1 The disagreement →

  2. Design three bridging metrics

    What properties of a comment might help opposing sides understand each other? Define three metrics and explain how you would identify them.

    2 Design metrics →

  3. Test your metrics

    Apply them to a sample of approximately 20 comments, manually or with LLM assistance. Which comments would your metrics prioritise, compared with ranking by likes?

    3 Code a sample → · 3 Compare with likes →

  4. Reflect on the intervention

    What might your proposed ranking make more visible? What assumptions does it make about productive disagreement, and what might it overlook?

Submission

Submit a short methods note (maximum 500 words) accompanied by your three metrics and a small table or spreadsheet showing your coded comments.

You may work individually or in groups of up to three students. If you work in a group, you may submit the same jointly prepared assignment.

Everyone must upload the assignment individually to Canvas, even when working in a group. Please include the names of your group members, if applicable.

On this site, the report on 3 Compare with likes (Word or PDF) contains your three metrics and a table of your coded comments; the CSV download on the same page is the spreadsheet. Your work is saved in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.
