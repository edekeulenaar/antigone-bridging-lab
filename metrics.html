2 · Design three bridging metrics

What properties of a comment might help opposing sides understand each other? Define three metrics and explain how you would identify them. For each, write a name, what it captures, and how you identify it: exactly when a comment counts as “yes”, precise enough that two people, or a person and an LLM, would code the same comment the same way.

Examples: a comment has “breadth” if it makes two or more different statements; or it gives context if it explains a statement with a source, a date or an explanation.

Your metrics are saved in this browser and can be downloaded as a Word document or a PDF; they are also part of the report on 3 Compare with likes.
