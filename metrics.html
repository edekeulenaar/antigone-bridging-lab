3 · Define your bridging metrics

Define three metrics. These should be properties of a comment that, if comments with them came first, would help other sides understand each other. For each, write:

  • a name;
  • what it captures, and why putting such comments first would amount to “bridging”;
  • a coding rule: exactly when a comment counts as “yes”, precise enough that two people, or a person and a chatbot, would code every comment the same way.

Examples: a comment has “breadth” if it makes two or more different statements; or it gives context if it explains a statement with a source, a date or an explanation.

Your codebook can be downloaded as a Word document or a PDF, and is also part of the report in step 6.
