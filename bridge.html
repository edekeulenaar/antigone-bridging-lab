Statements and clusters

How many comments make each statement, and the cluster each belongs to. Clusters group the statements that go together — one side's statements in one cluster, the other side's in another. Cross-cutting uses them. They are filled in from the Datasets' opinion groups when your file is one of its posts, and otherwise from the post and the fact-check: what the post says is cluster A, what the fact-check says is cluster B, the rest C. Change them if they do not fit.
