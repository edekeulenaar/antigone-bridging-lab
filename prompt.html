3 · Test your metrics on a sample

Apply your metrics to a sample of approximately 20 comments, manually or with LLM assistance. Then upload the coded sample on 3 Compare with likes to see which comments your metrics would prioritise, compared with ranking by likes.

a

Draw a sample

The sample is drawn at random and stays the same in this browser until you draw a new one. The file holds the original post and the fact-check for context, the sampled comments, and three empty columns, metric_A, metric_B and metric_C, for your codes.

The sample
b

Code it

Manually

Open the sample in a spreadsheet and write yes or no in metric_A, metric_B and metric_C for every comment, following how you identify each metric. Save it as CSV.

With LLM assistance

Open a chatbot (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Mistral, DeepSeek…), attach the sample and paste the prompt below. It fills in the three columns. Download the CSV it returns and check its codes; correct them where you disagree. If it prints the CSV as text, copy it into a plain-text editor and save it as .csv. Note the LLM you used.

Edit your metrics — they are saved in this browser, so the prompt below updates when you come back.
